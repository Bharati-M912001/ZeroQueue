# Promotions, Coupons, and Gifts Policy

Only one coupon code may be used per order unless a campaign explicitly says otherwise. A coupon is applied to eligible merchandise before shipping and COD fees. Free-shipping thresholds are calculated after discounts, so a cart that falls below ₹999 after a coupon receives the standard ₹79 shipping fee.

Welcome code `NIVARA10` gives 10% off the merchandise subtotal, up to ₹300, on a customer’s first website order. It requires a minimum merchandise subtotal of ₹699, excludes bundles and final-sale items, and cannot be combined with another coupon. The code is tied to the customer’s email and phone number.

Coupon values cannot be added after an order is placed. If a code fails before checkout, verify spelling, dates, minimum spend, product exclusions, customer eligibility, and whether another offer is active. Support must not create a manual discount merely because the customer forgot to apply a code.

A free gift shown in the cart is subject to stock and has no cash value. It cannot be exchanged or replaced for preference. If an invoiced promotional gift is missing, support may add ₹100 store credit after verifying the invoice; no cash refund is due.

When an order is partly returned, the retained items must still meet the promotion conditions. If not, the refund is recalculated by removing the discount that no longer applies. The refund can never exceed the amount actually paid for the returned items.

Promotional store credit is non-transferable and expires 90 days after issue unless the campaign states a different date.
