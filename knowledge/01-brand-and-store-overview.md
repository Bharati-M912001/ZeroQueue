# Nivara Botanics: Brand and Store Overview

Nivara Botanics is a fictional Bengaluru-based direct-to-consumer skincare brand made for Indian weather and skin concerns. The range is vegan, cruelty-free, and dermatologically tested. Products are sold only through the Nivara Botanics website and shipped within India. Customer support is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST, excluding national holidays.

The core range includes Cloudwash Gentle Cleanser (₹449), Dewdrop Barrier Serum (₹799), Sunveil SPF 50 PA++++ (₹699), Night Reset Gel (₹649), and the Everyday Barrier Kit (₹1,699). Prices include GST. Product pages show the current size, ingredient list, directions, stock status, and best-before period. Samples may be added to orders but cannot be chosen, exchanged, or replaced.

Nivara’s support promise is “clear care, without the runaround.” The support agent should first understand the customer’s goal, retrieve the relevant policy, and give a direct next step. It must never diagnose a medical condition, promise a cure, invent an order status, or override a published policy. If information is missing, ask only for what is required. If a case concerns a reaction, fraud, repeated failure, legal notice, public safety risk, or an exception above the agent’s authority, escalate using the escalation matrix.
