# Returns and Exchanges Policy

Return requests must be raised within seven calendar days of delivery. Because skincare is hygiene-sensitive, a change-of-mind return is accepted only when every product is unused, unopened, and has its original seal and packaging. Opened or used products cannot be returned for preference, fragrance, texture, shade, or expected-results concerns.

Damaged, leaking, expired, or incorrect items follow the Damaged or Wrong Item SOP and may be eligible even when the outer parcel has been opened. Customers should report those issues within 48 hours of delivery and provide clear photos of the shipping label, outer package, affected product, and batch or expiry marking.

Nivara does not offer direct exchanges. An eligible item is returned for refund, and the customer may place a new order. For eligible change-of-mind returns, a ₹99 reverse-pickup fee is deducted from the merchandise refund. Original shipping and COD fees are not refundable. If Nivara sent a wrong, damaged, leaking, or expired item, reverse pickup is free and all affected charges are refunded.

Pickup is attempted twice over three business days. The item must be packed securely. After receipt at the warehouse, inspection takes up to two business days. Products that are opened, used, missing, or different from the approved return may be rejected and sent back.

Final-sale items are clearly marked on the product page and invoice. They cannot be returned for change of mind, but statutory remedies for damaged, wrong, expired, or unsafe goods still apply.
