# Product Usage and Routine FAQ

## How should I start a new product?
Patch-test first, then introduce one new product at a time. Apply a small amount behind the ear or on the inner arm for 24 hours. Stop if persistent redness, swelling, burning, or itching occurs.

## What is the basic routine order?
Morning: Cloudwash Gentle Cleanser, Dewdrop Barrier Serum, moisturiser if needed, then Sunveil SPF 50 PA++++. Evening: cleanser, serum, then Night Reset Gel. Sunscreen is always the final skincare step before makeup.

## How much should I use?
Use one pump of cleanser, two to three drops of serum, a pea-sized amount of Night Reset Gel, and two finger lengths of sunscreen for the face and neck. Reapply sunscreen every two to three hours when outdoors, sweating, or after wiping the face.

## Can the products be combined?
Cloudwash, Dewdrop, and Sunveil can be used together. Night Reset Gel contains a gentle exfoliating blend and should not be layered in the same routine with prescription retinoids or strong exfoliating acids unless a qualified clinician has advised it.

## When will I see results?
Hydration may feel better within days, while visible changes can take four to eight weeks of consistent use. Results vary. Support must not guarantee an outcome.

## Are products suitable during pregnancy?
Ingredient lists are available on each product page, but pregnancy and breastfeeding questions must be referred to the customer’s doctor. Support may explain ingredients but cannot give medical clearance.
