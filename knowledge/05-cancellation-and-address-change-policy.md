# Cancellation and Address Change Policy

Customers may cancel an order at no charge while its status is Confirmed. They can use Account > Orders > Cancel Order or ask support to cancel after identity verification. Cancellation is no longer available once the status changes to Packed or Shipped, because the parcel has entered fulfilment.

For prepaid orders cancelled while Confirmed, the full paid amount, including shipping or COD fee if any was charged incorrectly, is returned to the original payment method. The refund is initiated within one business day and usually appears within five to seven business days. Bank processing time is outside Nivara’s control.

For a Packed or Shipped order, support must not promise cancellation. The customer may refuse delivery. After the parcel returns to Nivara and passes receipt inspection, the merchandise value is refunded. The original ₹79 shipping fee and ₹49 COD fee, if applicable, are not refundable for customer-requested refusal. If Nivara or the carrier caused the refusal, all fees are refunded.

An address, phone number, or recipient name can be corrected only while the order is Confirmed. Support may make one correction after verifying the order number and registered contact detail. Changing the delivery city or PIN code is treated as a cancellation and new order, not a simple correction.

Duplicate-order claims should be checked by comparing order numbers, items, amount, and timestamps. If both are still Confirmed, cancel the unwanted order. If either has progressed, follow the normal policy without inventing an exception.
