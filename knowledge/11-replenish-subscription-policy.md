# Replenish Subscription Policy

Nivara Replenish is an optional recurring delivery plan for eligible products. Customers choose delivery every 30, 45, or 60 days and receive 10% off eligible merchandise. Shipping is free when the discounted recurring merchandise subtotal is ₹999 or more; otherwise the standard ₹79 shipping fee applies. Cash on delivery is not available for subscriptions.

The first order is charged at signup. Later charges are attempted three calendar days before the scheduled dispatch date. Nivara sends a reminder email five calendar days before dispatch. Customers can skip, change frequency, update quantity, or cancel under Account > Replenish until 11:59 PM IST on the day before the charge attempt.

After a recurring order is charged, it follows the normal cancellation policy. It may be cancelled while Confirmed but not once Packed or Shipped. A failed recurring payment is retried once after 48 hours. If the retry fails, that cycle is skipped and the subscription remains active for the next scheduled date.

Subscription prices can change. Nivara provides at least seven calendar days’ notice before a higher price applies. Coupons cannot be used on recurring orders, but promotional store credit may be used only when the account settings explicitly allow it.

Returned subscription products follow the standard seven-day return and hygiene rules. Cancelling a subscription stops future orders; it does not cancel a separate order already created. Support may guide customers through account controls but must escalate disputed charges, missing cancellation records, or repeated billing after confirmed cancellation to Payments.
