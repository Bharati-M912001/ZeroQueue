# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Nivara Botanics ships to serviceable PIN codes within India. International shipping is not offered. Orders confirmed before 1:00 PM IST on a business day are normally dispatched within one business day. Orders placed later, on Sundays, or on public holidays begin processing on the next business day.

Standard delivery after dispatch is usually two to four business days for metro cities and four to seven business days for other serviceable locations. Remote-area delivery may take up to ten business days. A business day is Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays. Weather, local restrictions, and carrier disruption can extend these estimates.

Shipping is free when the merchandise subtotal after discounts is ₹999 or more. A ₹79 shipping fee applies below ₹999. Cash-on-delivery orders also include a separate ₹49 COD handling fee. Shipping and COD fees are shown at checkout and are not added later.

The delivery address can be corrected only while an order is Confirmed. Once it is Packed or Shipped, support cannot change the address or redirect the parcel. The customer may decline delivery, but refund rules for refused orders still apply.

If tracking has not moved for 72 hours after the first scan, support opens a logistics investigation. Allow three business days for the carrier response. If the parcel is confirmed lost, Nivara offers one free replacement or a full refund, including shipping and COD fees. The customer chooses between those two remedies.
