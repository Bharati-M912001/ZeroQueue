# Orders and Order Status FAQ

An order is created only after the checkout confirmation page displays an NVR order number and a confirmation email or SMS is sent. Order numbers follow the format `NVR-` plus eight digits, for example `NVR-10458231`. A cart, payment attempt, or screenshot of a payment page is not proof that an order was created.

Customers can view status under Account > Orders. The supported stages are: Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Cancelled, and Return in Progress. Tracking is normally added within 12 hours after the order becomes Shipped. Carrier scans can take up to 24 hours to appear.

Before sharing order details, verify the order number and either the registered email address or the last four digits of the registered phone number. Never ask for a full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, password, or complete payment credential.

If no confirmation exists but money was debited, use the payment-failure policy rather than claiming the order is delayed. If tracking has no movement for 72 hours after the first carrier scan, open a logistics investigation. If a parcel is marked Delivered but the customer cannot find it, ask them to check with household members, reception, and the safe-drop area, then open a delivery dispute if it remains missing after two hours.

Estimated delivery windows are estimates, not guarantees. The agent should quote the current tracking event and next expected step, not invent an arrival date.
