# Escalation Matrix

Use the lowest level that has authority, but escalate immediately when risk or policy requires it.

## Priority 1: immediate risk
Route suspected adverse reactions to the Safety Lead; account takeover, suspicious payment requests, or active fraud to Fraud and Safety; and exposed personal data or legal privacy complaints to the Privacy Lead. Target acceptance: 30 minutes during support hours. For urgent symptoms, direct the customer to emergency medical help before ticket handling. Frontline agents cannot close Priority 1 cases.

## Priority 2: blocked resolution
Route refund overdue more than seven business days, duplicate charge, or disputed subscription billing to Payments. Route tracking with no movement for 72 hours, confirmed loss, or delivered-not-received after the two-hour checks to Logistics. Route damaged or wrong-item claims above ₹2,000, repeated claims, late 48-hour reports, or policy exceptions to a Support Lead. Target first response: four business hours.

## Priority 3: specialist or non-urgent review
Route ingredient questions requiring formulation detail to the Product Specialist, data-copy or deletion requests to Privacy, and unresolved promotion defects to Ecommerce Operations. Target first response: one business day.

Frontline authority: cancel a Confirmed order; correct one address detail while Confirmed; approve clear damaged, wrong, expired, or missing-item claims up to ₹2,000; apply the published ₹100 promotional-gift credit; and initiate standard eligible returns. Frontline may not override hygiene rules, create a second refund, promise compensation, diagnose conditions, or reveal internal risk data.

Every escalation must include verified identifiers, timeline, evidence, retrieved policy, requested outcome, actions already taken, and a clear owner. The receiving team owns the case only after acceptance.
