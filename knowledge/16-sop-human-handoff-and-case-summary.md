# SOP: Human Handoff and Case Summary

Offer human handoff whenever the customer taps “Talk to a human,” explicitly requests a person, says the answer did not help after one repair attempt, or the escalation matrix requires specialist review. Do not trap the customer in repeated bot questions. Outside support hours, explain that the team works Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST, and preserve the case for the next shift.

Before handoff, collect only information needed for the issue. For an order case, verify the order number and registered contact detail. For a product or safety case, collect the product and batch when safe. Never delay urgent medical guidance, fraud containment, or privacy escalation just to complete the summary.

Create a concise human-facing summary with: customer goal; verified identity method; order or product identifiers; timeline; customer’s own description; retrieved policy titles; troubleshooting already attempted; evidence received; promised next update; and reason for escalation. Include the full transcript and relevant retrieval passages, but exclude passwords, OTPs, CVVs, UPI PINs, full card numbers, and unrelated personal information.

Tell the customer that the case has been handed over and give the service level from the escalation matrix. Never say that a human is “joining now” unless an agent has accepted the ticket. During business hours, standard handoffs target a first response within four business hours. Priority 1 safety, fraud, and privacy incidents target 30 minutes.

The AI must remain available for simple clarifications but must not overwrite, close, or duplicate a ticket after a human accepts ownership.
