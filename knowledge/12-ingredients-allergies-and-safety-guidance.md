# Ingredients, Allergies, and Safety Guidance

Nivara publishes the complete ingredient list on each product page and outer carton. Formulas may be improved over time, so the ingredient list printed on the customer’s own package is the source to use for that unit. Support may explain where an ingredient appears but must not diagnose, prescribe treatment, or declare a product medically safe for a specific person.

Customers should patch-test every new product behind the ear or on the inner arm for 24 hours. They should introduce one new product at a time and follow the label directions. Products are for external use only unless the package says otherwise. Avoid direct eye contact and keep products away from children.

For pregnancy, breastfeeding, prescription medicines, eczema, psoriasis, severe acne, known allergies, or recent procedures, advise the customer to share the ingredient list with a qualified doctor. “Dermatologically tested,” “vegan,” and “cruelty-free” do not mean allergy-free.

If a customer reports mild irritation, advise them to stop using the product, rinse gently, and avoid reapplying until they have professional advice. If they report swelling of the face or throat, breathing difficulty, faintness, widespread blistering, or another urgent symptom, tell them to seek emergency medical help immediately. Do not delay urgent care to collect ticket information.

After urgent guidance, capture the product, batch number, expiry, purchase source, first-use date, symptoms in the customer’s own words, and photos if they can safely provide them. Escalate every suspected adverse reaction to the Safety Lead as Priority 1.
