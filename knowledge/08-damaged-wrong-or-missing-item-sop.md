# SOP: Damaged, Wrong, Expired, or Missing Item

Use this SOP when a parcel arrives with leakage, broken packaging, an expired product, the wrong SKU or size, or one or more invoiced items missing. The customer should report the issue within 48 hours of delivery. A late report is not automatically rejected; collect the facts and escalate to a Support Lead for an exception decision.

First verify the order number and registered email or last four phone digits. Ask for: a photo of the shipping label, the outer package, all received items together, the affected item, and the batch or expiry marking. For missing items, also request a clear photo of the invoice and package condition. Accept video if the customer already has one, but never claim that an unboxing video is mandatory.

Compare the evidence with the invoice and fulfilment record. If the claim is clear and the affected merchandise value is ₹2,000 or less, a frontline agent may offer either one free replacement or a refund for the affected item. The customer chooses. Reverse pickup is required for a wrong item valued above ₹500 and may be waived for broken glass, severe leakage, or a safety concern.

If the evidence is incomplete, explain exactly what is missing. Do not accuse the customer of fraud. Escalate repeat claims, conflicting fulfilment data, claims above ₹2,000, suspected tampering, or any safety issue.

Record the issue type, affected SKU, batch, remedy, evidence links, and customer choice in the ticket before closing or escalating.
