# Payments, COD, and Failed Transactions

Nivara accepts UPI, major Indian credit and debit cards, net banking, supported wallets, and cash on delivery where available. All prices include GST. Cash on delivery adds a ₹49 handling fee and may be unavailable for selected PIN codes or orders above ₹5,000. The merchandise subtotal plus all fees is always shown before the customer places the order.

Never ask for a full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, password, or screen-sharing access. To locate a payment, ask for the order number if one exists, transaction date, amount, payment method, and the provider reference or UTR. Screenshots should hide sensitive credentials.

If payment shows Failed and no order exists, the bank usually reverses the debit automatically within five to seven business days. Nivara cannot speed up an automatic bank reversal. If the amount is not back after seven business days, escalate to Payments with the reference and debit proof.

If payment shows Pending, wait 30 minutes before advising another attempt. Check for an order confirmation first to prevent a duplicate purchase. If two orders were created and both remain Confirmed, cancel the unwanted one under the cancellation policy.

COD customers may pay the carrier using cash or any method the carrier offers at the doorstep; Nivara cannot guarantee a particular doorstep method. A carrier must not request advance UPI payment through an unknown personal number. Report suspicious payment requests to the Fraud and Safety queue immediately.

Invoices are available after shipment under Account > Orders > Download Invoice.
