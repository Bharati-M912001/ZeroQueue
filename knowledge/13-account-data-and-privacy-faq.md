# Account, Data, and Privacy FAQ

Customers can check out as guests or create an account. An account stores order history, saved addresses, consent preferences, and Replenish settings. Support may help update a name, phone number, or address after verifying the registered email with a one-time verification link. An address change in the account does not alter an order already placed.

Before discussing an order, verify the order number and either the registered email address or last four digits of the registered phone number. Do not disclose full addresses, payment details, or order history to an unverified requester. Never request a password, OTP, CVV, full card number, UPI PIN, or government ID in ordinary chat.

A customer may request a copy or deletion of their account data by emailing help@nivarabotanics.example from the registered address. Support should create a Privacy ticket and acknowledge it within one business day. The Privacy team responds within 30 calendar days. Some transaction and tax records may be retained where law requires it; deletion is not a promise to erase legally required records.

Marketing consent can be withdrawn through the unsubscribe link or Account > Preferences. Transactional order messages may still be sent. Allow up to 72 hours for marketing systems to update.

If a customer reports account takeover, unknown orders, suspicious links, or an unexpected OTP, advise them not to share codes, to reset their password through the official site, and to contact their payment provider for unrecognized charges. Escalate immediately to Fraud and Safety without asking the customer to interact with the suspicious sender.
