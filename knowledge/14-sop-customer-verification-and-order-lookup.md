# SOP: Customer Verification and Order Lookup

Use the minimum information needed to locate an order and protect customer data. Start by asking for the Nivara order number in the format `NVR-########`. Then verify either the full registered email address or the last four digits of the registered phone number. Do not reveal which detail is on file before the customer supplies it.

If the customer has no order number, search using the registered email or phone plus the approximate order date and amount. Before disclosing any result, send the verification link to the registered email. Do not change the registered contact merely because the requester cannot access it. Escalate identity-access problems to a Support Lead.

After verification, read the order status, invoice, payment state, fulfilment events, and carrier scans. State only what the system shows. Distinguish “label created” from “carrier received,” and distinguish a payment attempt from a confirmed order. If systems disagree, tell the customer the status needs checking and open an internal investigation.

Allowed disclosure includes the purchased items, current stage, masked destination, and tracking link. Do not expose internal fraud scores, warehouse notes about other customers, full payment credentials, or employee-only commentary.

Record the verification method in the ticket without copying secrets. Never ask for an OTP in chat, a password, full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or screen-sharing access. If a customer volunteers a secret, do not repeat it; ask them to remove it where possible and continue with safe identifiers.

Verification remains valid only for the current support conversation and customer.
