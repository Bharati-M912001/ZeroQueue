# Refunds and Store Credit Policy

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Nivara does not ask customers to share card numbers, UPI PINs, OTPs, or bank passwords to receive a refund. Refund initiation and bank credit are different events: support should provide the initiation date and reference number when available, without guaranteeing the bank’s posting date.

For a cancelled Confirmed order, the refund is initiated within one business day. For an approved return, it is initiated within one business day after warehouse inspection. Once initiated, prepaid card, UPI, net-banking, and wallet refunds usually appear within five to seven business days. COD refunds are paid by bank transfer or UPI within five to seven business days after the customer submits a verified payout destination through the secure refund form.

A change-of-mind return receives the merchandise value minus the ₹99 reverse-pickup fee. Original ₹79 shipping and ₹49 COD fees are not refunded. For a wrong, damaged, leaking, expired, lost, or Nivara-caused order, the approved affected amount includes applicable shipping, COD, and reverse-pickup fees.

Promotional store credit is non-transferable and expires 90 days after issue. Store credit used on a returned order is restored with its original expiry date or 30 days from restoration, whichever is later. Cash cannot replace promotional credit.

If seven business days have passed since refund initiation, verify the refund reference and escalate to Payments. A refund can be reissued only after Payments confirms failure or reversal; support must not create a second refund on assumption.
